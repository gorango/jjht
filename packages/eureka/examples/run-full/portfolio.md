# ArmaturaSec d.o.o. × Krisztina Kiss

**Challenge:** We need to detect unusual behaviour on Modbus and BACnet networks without labelled attack data — our clients have never been breached (or don't know it), so we have zero examples of malicious traffic and supervised classifiers are useless to us.

## The problem
detect unusual behaviour on Modbus and BACnet networks without labelled attack data — our clients have never been breached (or don't know it), so we have zero examples of malicious traffic and supervised classifiers are useless to us.

## Research questions
1. Which part of the stated problem can be solved with the methods the faculty already uses?
2. What measurement would convince the company that the result works on its own line?
3. What would make the approach fail badly enough to stop after year one?

## Plan
| Year | Work | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A baseline built on 2,000 labelled samples from the company's existing process | A working baseline and a dataset paper |
| 2 | Extend the method to the second operating condition and measure it on site | A journal paper and a prototype |
| 3 | A full trial through one operating cycle, compared with the current process | The thesis and a rollout decision |

## What each side brings
- **Company:** We can dedicate one to two engineers part-time to a collaborative project, with access to our traffic archives, our hardware bench, and our existing firmware codebase under NDA. and access to the operating data.
- **Faculty:** supervision by Krisztina Kiss, laboratory time, and anomaly detection, one-class classification, industrial control systems.

## Why this pair
Shared ground with the company: modbus, networks, data, traffic. Krisztina Kiss's paper "Statistical Baseline Modelling of Modbus/TCP Traffic in a Substation Automation Network" (SYN-PUB-10472) is the closest published work.

## Agreed up front
- Results may be published after a 60-day review by the company.
- The company owns the rig and the trained models; the faculty keeps the methods.

## Risks
The pair fails if the company cannot commit timely access to its operating data.

## Unverified evidence
- Budget, data-access terms and the candidate's availability are not in the dataset and remain to be confirmed.

## Supervisors and candidate
- **Academic supervisor:** Krisztina Kiss (Full Professor, Department of Computing and Control Engineering).
- **Industrial supervisor:** Radovan Čičić (Head Of Engineering, ArmaturaSec d.o.o.).
- **Candidate:** a master's graduate in Industrial cybersecurity, employed by ArmaturaSec d.o.o. for the three years.
