# Event — dates, venue, schedule

Source: <https://build.jigjoy.ai/> and <https://jigjoy.ai/hackathon-2026> (accessed 2026-10-30).

## Identity

- **Name:** NTP Novi Sad Hackathon — "Build agents that work together" (edition: 30–31 October 2026)
- **Organizer:** Jig Joy D.O.O. Novi Sad, Fruškogorska 1, 21000 Novi Sad, Serbia
- **General partner:** Science and Technology Park Novi Sad (NTP NS)
- **Partner/project:** EINSTEIN (EU project) — source of Track 02's framing and data
- **Sponsors:** mem0 (current edition); last edition also daily.dev, Hyperskill, Adaption
- **Community partner:** Beaver (beaver.rs)

## Format

- Runs **on site** at NTP Novi Sad and **online**, in parallel.
- Online follows the **same schedule, same theme, same jury** as on-site.
- On-site teams get a desk, coffee, and mentors within reach for both days.
- The kickoff and brief on Friday 10:00 are **streamed for the online track**.
- "48 h kickoff to submission" is the marketing framing; the actual window is Friday 10:00 →
  Saturday 14:00 (≈28 h). See `10-source-log.md`.

## Registration

- Two modes: **on site** (`/apply?mode=offline`) and **online** (`/apply?mode=online`).
- Registration closes **09:00 on 30 October**.
- Form asks: name, email, taking part (on site / online), entering as (solo / team), and an optional
  marketing opt-in for future JigJoy events and partner offers.
- Solo entrants are offered help finding teammates.
- Every registrant is promised the docs, a starter template, and a primer before kickoff, plus
  mentors during the event.
