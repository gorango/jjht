# Prizes

Sources: landing page, `/rules` §7–§9 (accessed 2026-10-30).

## Cash

| Place | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | **$500** |
| 2nd | **$300** |
| 3rd | **$200** |
| **Total pool** | **$1,000** |

- Awarded to the **top three overall**, regardless of track.
- Paid **through Upwork**. The winner, or a team's **designated representative**, needs an Upwork
  account able to receive payments and must supply the information needed to complete payment.
- A team's prize goes to its representative; splitting it is the team's own decision.
- JigJoy initiates payment **within 7 days** of the winner announcement, extendable if the winner is
  unresponsive, missing paperwork, ineligible, or unable to receive payment via Upwork.

## Non-cash

- **5× six-month Mozaik Cloud Premium** subscriptions for the first five teams
  (<https://mozaik.jigjoy.ai>).
- **3× two-week unpaid AI internships** in Novi Sad, for IT students and junior developers who can
  attend in person. Awarded by JigJoy.
- Last edition's five-place table also included **daily.dev Plus (12 months)** and **Hyperskill
  Premium (6 months)**; those were September-edition sponsor prizes and are not promised for this
  edition.

## Taxes

- JigJoy handles the administrative, legal, and tax obligations around **its distribution of the
  cash prizes**.
- Tax on **non-cash prizes** (including Cloud Premium subscriptions) is the recipient's
  responsibility, except where local law says otherwise.
- Governing law is Serbia; disputes go to Belgrade courts after good-faith negotiation.

## Practical note

If a cash prize is realistic, decide **now** who the team's designated representative is and make
sure that person has a working Upwork account. It is a silly way to lose $500.
