# Official rules

Transcribed from <https://build.jigjoy.ai/rules> (accessed 2026-10-30; page says "Last updated
19 September 2026"). Wording preserved; formatting adapted. The online page is authoritative.

The NTP Novi Sad Hackathon (the "Hackathon") is organized by Jig Joy D.O.O. Novi Sad,
Fruškogorska 1, 21000 Novi Sad, Serbia ("JigJoy"), in partnership with Science and Technology Park
Novi Sad ("NTP NS"), the general partner. It runs on site at NTP NS and online. By registering for
the Hackathon or submitting a project you agree to these rules.

---

## 1. Schedule

- The Hackathon runs on **30–31 October 2026**, on site at NTP NS and online, with an introductory
  Mozaik stream on **29 October**.
- Submissions close **Saturday, 31 October 2026 at 14:00 CET**. You can resubmit until the
  deadline — the latest entry is the one judged.
- Winners are announced on **7 November 2026**, in Discord and by email.
- Schedule details, links, and operational updates are announced on the participant dashboard, in
  Discord, and by email.

## 2. Eligibility

- Open **worldwide**. You must be at least **18 years old**, or the age of majority in your
  jurisdiction, whichever is higher.
- Participation is void where prohibited by law.
- People directly involved in organizing or judging may participate, but **cannot win prizes**.

## 3. Teams

- Enter solo or in a team of **up to 4 people**.
- **One entry per team**, and a person can be on **one team only**.

## 4. Projects

- Your project must be **built during the Hackathon**. Pre-existing frameworks, libraries, and
  boilerplate may be used, but **the substance of the work must happen during the event**.
- The project must use **Mozaik (`@mozaik-ai/core`)** and run **two (2) or more concurrent agents**.
- **AI-assisted development is allowed and encouraged.**
- You must own, or have the right to use, everything you submit — code, assets, and data. No
  infringing, unlawful, or harmful content.

## 5. Submissions

- Submit through the submission page: **project name**, a **repository the judges can access**, a
  **description**, and an **explanation of how your agents run concurrently**.
- A demo video, live deployment, and screenshots are **optional but strongly recommended**.
- Submissions the judges **cannot open, run, or verify** may be excluded from judging.

## 6. Judging

- To be eligible, a project must **run two or more agents on Mozaik** — a single-agent project does
  not qualify.
- Submissions are judged by a **panel selected by JigJoy**.
- The weight is on **genuine use of concurrency — agents that actually run together** — alongside
  technical execution, creativity, and the clarity of the submission.
- Judging takes up to **7 days** after submissions close, and may be extended by up to **7
  additional days** if the number of submissions requires it.
- **Judging decisions are final.**

## 7. Prizes

- Cash prize pool of **USD $1,000**: First Place **$500**, Second Place **$300**, Third Place
  **$200**.
- JigJoy provides **five (5) six-month Mozaik Cloud Premium subscriptions** for winning
  participants.
- Any further prizes announced by sponsors before the kickoff are fulfilled by those sponsors and
  subject to their reasonable account and eligibility requirements.

## 8. Cash prize payment — Upwork

- Cash prizes are distributed **through Upwork**.
- To receive a cash prize, the winner — or, for a team, its **designated representative** — needs an
  Upwork account able to receive payments, and must provide the information reasonably required to
  complete the payment.
- A team's cash prize is paid to its designated representative; how it is split within the team is
  the team's own decision.
- JigJoy contacts winners and initiates payment **within 7 days** of the winner announcement. This
  period extends where a winner is unresponsive, does not provide the required information, does not
  satisfy these rules, or cannot receive payment through Upwork.

## 9. Taxes

- JigJoy handles the administrative, legal, and tax obligations associated with its distribution of
  the cash prizes.
- Any tax obligations related to **non-cash prizes** — including the Mozaik Cloud Premium
  subscriptions — are the responsibility of the prize recipient, except where applicable law
  requires otherwise.

## 10. Your work stays yours

- You keep all intellectual property in your project.
- By submitting, you grant JigJoy and NTP NS a **non-exclusive right to present your project** — its
  name, description, screenshots, and demo — in the public gallery, winner announcements, and
  event-related communications.

## 11. Conduct

- Be respectful in Discord, on streams, and everywhere the event happens.
- JigJoy may **disqualify** a participant or team for plagiarism, misrepresentation of authorship,
  harassment, sabotage of other teams or of the event, or any material breach of these rules.

## 12. Data & privacy

- Participant data collected through registration and submission is handled by JigJoy in line with
  the JigJoy privacy policy.
- **Names of on-site participants are shared with NTP NS for venue access; nothing else is shared**
  with partners or sponsors.

## 13. Changes

- JigJoy may make reasonable operational adjustments to the schedule, judging process, or prize
  fulfillment where necessary.
- **Material changes are announced through the dashboard, Discord, and email.**

## 14. Governing law

- Governed by the laws of the **Republic of Serbia**.
- Disputes are first addressed through good-faith negotiation; failing that, the competent courts
  in **Belgrade, Republic of Serbia**, have jurisdiction.

## Reading the rules for this team

Three clauses matter more than the rest:

1. **§4 + §6 "two or more concurrent agents"** — a hard eligibility gate, not a scoring preference.
   A single agent with many tools does not qualify.
2. **§5 "cannot open, run, or verify may be excluded"** — this is why the README, the offline mock
   run, and the lockfile matter as much as the architecture.
